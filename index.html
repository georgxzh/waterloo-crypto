[02] RESEARCH LAYER BLOCK TIME: FOREVER

Waterloo Consensus

Research. Academic. Built to outlast us. Workshops, recorded talks, a podcast and published papers, all content-addressed and signed by their authors. Members graduate, but their work stays on the chain.

  1. 01

    Workshops

    From elliptic curves to rollup design. Each session is recorded, versioned and published under an open license.

  2. 02

    Podcast EP.00 SOON

    Long-form conversations with researchers, founders and alumni. Every episode is pinned to IPFS.

  3. 03

    Published Papers

    Student research, reviewed by peers. Authorship is soulbound to each author's ENS, so credit can't be taken away.

  4. 04

    Hall of Fame

    Every past exec gets a card, minted as an NFT and held in their wallet for good.
